# **Critique Manifest — Zero‑Point Capture Layer**

Critique is the **first contact surface** of the TriadicFrameworks pipeline.  
It is where an idea is expressed for the first time in a structured environment.

Critique does not evaluate the idea.  
It **reveals** it.

Critique extracts:

- the core intent  
- the structural seed  
- the conceptual boundaries  
- the early paradox signals  
- the clarity surface  

This extraction becomes the **clarity packet**, which is passed directly into **Gradient** for alignment.

---

## **1. Purpose**

Critique answers one question:

> *What is the idea, in its clearest possible form?*

It provides:

- a clean submission surface  
- an initial clarity pass  
- a normalized idea object  
- a stable handoff into Gradient  

---

## **2. Position in the Pipeline**

Critique sits at the very start of the RTT lineage:

```
Critique → Gradient → RTT/1 → RTT/2 → RTT/3 → RTT/12
```

It is the **zero‑point capture layer**.

---

## **3. Core Functions**

### **(1) Expression Capture**  
Critique receives the raw idea exactly as the user expresses it.

### **(2) Clarity Extraction**  
Critique reveals the idea’s:

- structure  
- resonance  
- energy  
- contradictions  
- boundaries  

### **(3) Normalization**  
Critique produces a normalized clarity object.

### **(4) Handoff**  
Critique sends the clarity packet to Gradient for alignment.

---

## **4. Clarity Packet**

The clarity packet is the primary output of Critique.

```
clarity_packet:
  title: <idea title>
  summary: <short description>
  intent: <what the idea wants>
  contradictions: <early paradox signals>
  boundaries: <explicit limits>
  clarity_surface: <normalized clarity object>
```

Gradient uses this packet to begin S/R/E alignment.

---

## **5. Interaction with S/R/E Modes**

Critique supports the triad:

### **S‑mode (Structure)**  
Reveals the idea’s frame.

### **R‑mode (Resonance)**  
Reveals conceptual frequencies.

### **E‑mode (Energy)**  
Reveals activation forces.

Critique does not *align* these modes — it exposes them.

---

## **6. Handoff to Gradient**

The clarity packet is passed directly into:

**Gradient — Alignment Layer**  
`gradient/docs/attunement_template.md`

Gradient uses the packet to:

- stabilize coherence  
- bound drift  
- declare paradox  
- prepare the idea for RTT/1
