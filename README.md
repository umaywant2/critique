# **critique — Submit your idea. Get clarity.**

Critique is the **entry point** of the TriadicFrameworks pipeline.

It is the place where an idea is first expressed, captured, and prepared for alignment.  
Critique does not transform the idea — it **reveals** it.

Critique provides:

- a clean submission surface  
- an initial clarity pass  
- a normalized idea object  
- a handoff into **Gradient** for alignment  

This ensures every idea begins with a stable, structured foundation before entering RTT/1.

---

## **Purpose**

Critique answers one question:

> *What is the idea, in its clearest possible form?*

It extracts:

- the core intent  
- the structural seed  
- the conceptual boundaries  
- the immediate contradictions  
- the clarity surface  

This becomes the raw material for Gradient’s S/R/E alignment modes.

---

## **Pipeline Position**

Critique sits at the very start of the RTT lineage:

```
Critique → Gradient → RTT/1 → RTT/2 → RTT/3 → RTT/12
```

It is the **zero‑point capture layer**.

---

## **Output**

Critique produces a **clarity packet**:

```
clarity_packet:
  title: <idea title>
  summary: <short description>
  intent: <what the idea wants>
  contradictions: <early paradox signals>
  boundaries: <explicit limits>
  clarity_surface: <normalized clarity object>
```

This packet is passed directly into **Gradient** for alignment.
